Parts beyond what you have

    Assumptions

    • Raspberry Pi 5 in the official case (9.4 × 6.3 × 3.1 cm). A Pi 4 has the same footprint and the same USB-C side.
    • 2.5" SATA SSDs in USB enclosures (12.6 × 8 × 1.4 cm), one per Pi. NVMe sticks fit the same spots.
    • Wall outlets at the standard 30 cm height, behind the right half of the table. Move them in your head if yours differ; the riser stays the same.
    • FRITZ!Box 6660 Cable rear port order is approximate. Coax, power and LAN all land on the back, which is what the routing needs.
    • Table: panel thickness 1.5 cm and every edge position read from your IkeaHOLMERUD.skp.
